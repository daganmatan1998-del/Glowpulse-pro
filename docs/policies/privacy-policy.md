## What we collect

**When you visit the site:** your browser and device type, IP address, time zone, the pages you view and how you arrived. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies.

**When you place an order:** your name, billing address, shipping address, email address, phone number and payment details.

**When you contact us or subscribe:** your name, email address and whatever you choose to tell us in your message.

We do not see or store your full card number. Payments are processed by Shopify and the payment providers you choose at checkout.

## Why we use it

- To process, fulfil and deliver your order
- To send you order confirmations, shipping updates and tracking information
- To answer your questions and handle returns
- To send marketing emails, but only if you have opted in — every email has an unsubscribe link
- To screen orders for fraud
- To understand how the site is used so we can improve it
