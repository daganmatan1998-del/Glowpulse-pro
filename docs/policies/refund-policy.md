## The short version

You have **30 days from delivery** to tell us you want to return something. Email {{ support@yourstore.com }} with your order number and we will take it from there.

## Return window

Requests must be made within 30 days of the date your order was delivered. After 30 days we are unable to offer a refund or exchange.

To be eligible for a return, the item must be:

- In the same condition you received it — unworn, unused and undamaged
- In its original packaging, with any tags still attached
- Accompanied by proof of purchase (your order number is enough)

## How to start a return

1. Email {{ support@yourstore.com }} with your order number and a short note about why you are returning the item.
2. We reply within one business day with the return address and instructions.
3. Ship the item back and send us the tracking number.
4. Once the carrier scans the parcel, we process your refund.

**Please do not send anything back before contacting us.** Returns sent to an address that we have not given you cannot be processed or refunded.

## Who pays for return shipping

- **Faulty, damaged or wrong item:** we pay. You will not be out of pocket.
- **Changed your mind:** you pay the return shipping cost. Original shipping charges, if any, are not refunded.

## Refunds

Once your return is on its way back to us, we issue your refund within **3 business days** to the original payment method. Your bank or card issuer then typically takes a further **5 to 10 business days** to show the money in your account.

We will email you when the refund has been sent.

## Exchanges

The fastest way to exchange an item is to return the original for a refund and place a new order for the one you want. That way you are not waiting for your return to arrive before the replacement ships.

## Damaged or incorrect items

Check your order as soon as it arrives. If anything is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, email us within **14 days of delivery** with your order number and photos. We will send a replacement or refund you in full, and you will not need to pay any return postage.

## Items we cannot accept back

Unless the item is faulty, we cannot accept returns of:

- Personalised or custom-made items
- Perishable goods
- Intimate or sanitary products, and cosmetics where the seal has been broken
- Gift cards
- Items returned more than 30 days after delivery

## Cancelling an order

If your order has not yet been dispatched, email us and we will cancel it and refund you in full. Once an order has been dispatched, it falls under the returns process above.

## Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects your legal rights as a consumer. If you are in the {{ EU or UK }}, you have a statutory right to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it, for any reason, and this applies in addition to the 30-day window we offer above.

## Questions

Email {{ support@yourstore.com }}. A real person answers, usually within one business day.
