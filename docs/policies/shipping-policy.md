## Where we ship

We ship to most countries worldwide. If your country is not selectable at checkout, we are unfortunately unable to deliver there at the moment.

## Processing time

Orders are processed within **1 to 3 business days** of payment. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day. During sales periods and around major holidays, processing can take a little longer.

You will receive a confirmation email as soon as your order is placed, and a second email with tracking information once it has been dispatched.

## Delivery times

Delivery times are estimates from the day your order is dispatched, not the day you order.

| Destination | Standard delivery | Express delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United States | 7 to 14 business days | 4 to 8 business days |
| Canada | 8 to 16 business days | 5 to 9 business days |
| United Kingdom and Europe | 7 to 14 business days | 4 to 8 business days |
| Australia and New Zealand | 9 to 18 business days | 5 to 10 business days |
| Rest of the world | 10 to 20 business days | 7 to 14 business days |

These are working days and exclude weekends and public holidays. Delays caused by customs, extreme weather, carrier backlogs or incorrect addresses are outside our control.

## Where your order ships from

We work with fulfilment partners in {{ China, the United States and Europe }}. Your order is dispatched from whichever warehouse can reach you fastest, which is why items in the same order sometimes arrive in separate parcels on different days. Each parcel gets its own tracking number.

## Tracking your order

You will receive a tracking link by email once your order ships. Tracking information can take **3 to 5 business days** to start updating after dispatch — this is normal and does not mean your parcel is lost.

If tracking has not updated in 10 business days, email us at {{ support@yourstore.com }} with your order number and we will chase the carrier for you.

## Customs, duties and import taxes

Prices on our site do not include import duties or taxes.

For most destinations, no additional charge applies. If your country does levy import duty on parcels of this value, that charge is set by your customs authority, is payable by you as the recipient, and is not something we collect or control. Please check your local rules before ordering if you are unsure.

Refusing to pay import charges, which results in the parcel being returned or destroyed, is not grounds for a refund.

## Incorrect addresses

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If your order has not yet been dispatched, email us immediately and we will correct it.

Once an order has shipped, we cannot change the address. Parcels returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address can be reshipped once you pay the return and reshipping cost.

## Lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel is confirmed lost by the carrier, or arrives damaged, contact us within **14 days of the delivery date** with your order number and photos of the damage. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer, at no cost to you.
